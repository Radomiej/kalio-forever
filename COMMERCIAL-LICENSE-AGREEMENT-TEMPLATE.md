- **Documentation** means the Kalio documentation supplied by the Licensor or published with the Software.

The default grant is for internal use only. Redistribution, resale, embedding in a Licensed Product, white-labeling, hosted service, customer delivery, and sublicensing are excluded unless expressly selected in the Order Form.

Kalio is local-first. Unless the Order Form states otherwise, the Licensor does not receive or process the Licensee's application data, prompts, files, credentials, or execution history as part of ordinary desktop use.

If the Licensor processes personal data on behalf of the Licensee through hosting, support access, telemetry, or another service, the Parties must document their controller/processor roles and execute any required data processing agreement before that processing begins. The Licensor is not a party to the Licensee's contracts with those providers and does not warrant their availability, processing terms, retention, location, or output.

Each Party will protect the other Party's non-public information using reasonable care and will use it only to perform or receive the services under this Agreement. These confidentiality duties do not apply to information that is public, independently developed, already lawfully known, or lawfully received without a duty of confidentiality.

## 12. Term, termination, and effects

The Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the Term in the Order Form. Either Party may terminate for a material breach that remains uncured **[30]** days after written notice.
