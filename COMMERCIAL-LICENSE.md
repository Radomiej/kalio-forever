# Kalio commercial licensing

Kalio is source-available under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](./LICENSE).

The public license covers noncommercial use. A separate commercial license from the copyright holder is required when Kalio is used for a commercial purpose, including:

- use in a revenue-generating business or internal business tool;
- embedding Kalio or a derivative into a paid product;
- redistributing, reselling, or white-labeling Kalio;
- offering Kalio as a hosted, managed, or paid service; or
- delivering Kalio or a derivative to a client for compensation.

The exact rights, permitted users, support, updates, and commercial terms are defined by the separate commercial agreement. This document is a public policy summary, not that agreement.

Commercial licensing is handled individually. Start by sending the following information through a private channel to [Radomiej on GitHub](https://github.com/Radomiej):

- legal name and country of the licensee;
- whether the use is internal, embedded, redistributed, white-label, or hosted;
- number of users, installations, products, and affiliates;
- requested territory and license term;
- support, updates, SLA, and security-review requirements; and
- whether Kalio will process personal data on behalf of the licensee.

The normal process is: private intake, written quote and order form, agreement signed by both parties, payment, and written confirmation of the granted scope. Do not include secrets, customer data, or confidential architecture details in public GitHub issues.

The agreement template is [COMMERCIAL-LICENSE-AGREEMENT-TEMPLATE.md](./COMMERCIAL-LICENSE-AGREEMENT-TEMPLATE.md).
